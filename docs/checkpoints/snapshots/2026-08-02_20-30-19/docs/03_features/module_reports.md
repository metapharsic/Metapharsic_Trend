# Module: Business Intelligence Reports

This module catalogs the data compilation scopes for SFA, CRM, and Financial reporting.

---

## 1. Operational BI Reports
- **Target Audience**: Field Reps, Managers.
- **Reports**: Daily DCR logs, check-in timestamps, monthly doctor coverage percentages, and missed visit reminders.

---

## 2. Commercial & Sales Reports
- **Target Audience**: Marketing, Area Managers.
- **Reports**: Product SKU sales histories, territory performance rankings, and distributor inventory aging sheets.

---

## 3. Compliance & Audit Reports
- **Target Audience**: Security Officers, Admin.
- **Reports**: Out-of-geofence visit logs, mock location provider suspended check-ins, and sample/gift allocations compared against doctor cap quotas.
