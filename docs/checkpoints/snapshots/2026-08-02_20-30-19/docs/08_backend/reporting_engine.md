# Advanced Reporting Engine

Pharma OS leverages a dynamic BI engine to replace static reports with actionable analytics.

---

## 1. Reporting Levels

### Level 1: Operational Reports (Daily)
- **Target**: MRs, ASMs.
- **Includes**: Checked-in doctors, DCR summaries, travel distance tracked, orders captured.

### Level 2: Performance Reports (Monthly)
- **Target**: ASMs, RMs, HR.
- **Includes**: KPI target vs achievement, behavioral scores, expense total summaries, sample ROI.

### Level 3: Strategic Reports (Quarterly)
- **Target**: ZSMs, NSMs, Marketing.
- **Includes**: Competitor activity trends, campaign efficacy, regional market share adjustments.

### Level 4: Predictive Reports (AI-Based)
- **Target**: MD, NSMs, Supply Chain.
- **Includes**: Future territory-wise demand forecasts, stock-out predictions, MR flight/churn risk.

---

## 2. Dynamic BI Features
- **Heatmaps**: Territory mapping displaying doctor visit frequency and order booking concentrations.
- **Coverage Gap Analysis**: Visual maps showing under-serviced clinics or hospital territories.
- **Drill-down**: Toggle directly from National -> Zone -> Region -> Area -> MR -> Doctor level in dashboard panels.

---

## 3. The 100+ Reports Matrix

The BI engine provides a library of structured reports categorized by business function:

### A. Sales & Commercial Reports
- **Product-wise Sales**: Monthly units, revenue, and margins per product SKU.
- **Territory Performance**: Target vs Actual revenue rankings.
- **Growth Analysis**: MoM and YoY growth indicators per territory and region.
- **Target Gap Reports**: Real-time deficit listings showing areas lagging behind targets.

### B. Activity & Field Force Reports
- **DCR Summaries**: Daily log of completed, aborted, or missed calls.
- **Doctor Coverage Index**: Percentage of territory doctors visited in current cycle.
- **Missed Visit Logs**: Listing of high-priority doctors not visited within their DPS frequency schedule.
- **Call Average Reports**: Running daily calls per MR.

### C. Financial & Outstanding Reports
- **Accounts Receivable aging**: Overdue collection items for chemists and stockists.
- **Expense Claim Summaries**: Aggregated expenses per employee, territory, and category.
- **Sample ROI Reports**: Financial output correlates of sample distribution.

### D. Compliance & Audit Reports
- **GPS Violations**: Logs out-of-geofence check-ins.
- **Biometric Failures**: Selfie face check-in match failures.
- **Fake GPS Alerts**: Hardware spoof coordinates signatures detected.
- **Sample/Gift Audits**: Allocations compared against Doctor cap quotas.
