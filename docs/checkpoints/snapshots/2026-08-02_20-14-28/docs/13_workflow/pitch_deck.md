# Investor Pitch Deck (Text Edition)

**Project Name**: Pharma OS  
**Sub-title**: The Commercial Intelligence Platform for Global Pharma Enterprise  

---

## Slide 1: The Problem
Pharmaceutical sales operations are highly fragmented and inefficient:
- **Data Silos**: Companies use one app for attendance (HRMS), another for expense claims, and another for sales tracking. None of these talk to each other.
- **Field Fraud**: Inaccurate Daily Call Reports, fake GPS locations, and duplicate expense receipts cost companies millions.
- **Blind Visits**: Medical Representatives (MRs) visit doctors based on habit, rather than potential, resulting in low conversion rates and wasted sample distributions.

---

## Slide 2: The Solution (Pharma OS)
A unified Commercial Excellence Suite designed as the operating system for pharma companies:
- **Consolidated Modules**: Integrates SFA + CRM + HRMS + Expense Management into one cohesive stack.
- **AI-Powered Field Guard**: Automates fraud detection (biometric attendance, GPS geofencing verification, OCR bill duplicate tracking).
- **Intelligent Recommendations**: AI-driven route optimization, smart doctor prioritization scoring, and auto-inventory suggestions.

---

## Slide 3: Market Size & Opportunity
- **TAM (Total Addressable Market)**: Global Pharma Sales & Marketing operations software market is valued at $12.4 Billion.
- **SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)**: Emerging markets SFA and CRM systems representing $3.2 Billion.
- **SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)**: Capturing 2% of SAM over 5 years, representing a $64 Million Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) opportunity.

---

## Slide 4: Business & Revenue Model
- **SaaS Subscription**: Per User Per Month SaaS pricing ranging from $15 (Basic SFA) to $45 (Enterprise with AI Optimization).
- **Add-on Services**: Predictve supply forecasting models and automated customer messaging integrations.
- **Implementation & Support Fees**: Setup, custom ERP connectors (SAP/Oracle), and customized analytics design.
