# Module: Product Catalog & Visual Aids

This document details central product catalogs, visual aid allocations, and pricing margins.

---

## 1. The Central Product Catalog

Every item in the catalog specifies:
- **Medical Composition**: Base molecules, strengths, and pack sizes (e.g., 10 tablets/strip).
- **Price Configurations**:
  - **MRP (Max Retail Price)**: Price to consumers.
  - **PTR (Price to Retailer)**: Price Chemists pay to Distributors.
  - **PTS (Price to Stockist)**: Price Distributors pay to the Company.
- **Therapy Segments**: Mapped segments (e.g., Gastroenterology) used to restrict detailing calls.

---

## 2. Visual Aids & e-Detailing
- **Asset Library**: Store visual materials (interactive PDFs, detailing videos, clinical case studies) in the `VisualAid` table.
- **Mobile Presenter**: MRs access and launch these visual aids directly within the mobile application during doctor presentations.
- **Engagement Logs**: The app captures presentation duration per page/slide to measure marketing effectiveness.

---

## 3. Competitor Price Matrix
- Logs competitor product details, base composition, active price structures, and detailing counter-arguments (e.g. *Why our formulation is safer*).
