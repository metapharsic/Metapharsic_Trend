# Module: AI Engine & Analytics

This document details the executive AI dashboard, predictive algorithms, and alert rules.

---

## 1. Executive AI Insights
- **Sales Demand Forecasts**: Uses time-series models to predict territory stock needs.
- **Specialty Decline Flags**: Alerts marketing when a product's prescription volume starts dropping within a specific specialty.
- **Doctor Loyalty Predictors**: Gauges probability of doctors switching to competitor brands.

---

## 2. Autonomous Alert Broadcasting
- Triggers notifications to ASM/RM dashboards:
  - Mock GPS location signatures matching coordinated visit logs.
  - Missed visit alerts for high-priority doctors.

---

## 3. Simulation & Optimization Models
- **Incentive Simulator**: Models total commission expenditures before finalizing yearly appraisal brackets.
- **Scheme Impact Analyzer**: Simulates gross margin variations when adjusting stockist discount structures.
