# SaaS Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

This GTM strategy outlines the playbook for launching Pharma OS in enterprise markets.

---

## 1. Target Audience Segmentation

### Tier 1: Mid-Market Pharma Companies (50 - 500 Field Reps)
- **Pain Points**: Outgrowing paper sheets and basic CRM tools; high leakage in expense claims.
- **Sales Cycle**: 3 - 6 months.
- **Entry Strategy**: Direct outreach to Head of Commercial Excellence or Chief Operating Officer.

### Tier 2: Large Enterprise Pharma (500+ Field Reps)
- **Pain Points**: Deep integration needs with existing ERP infrastructure (SAP, Oracle, Dynamics); compliance reporting bottlenecks across multiple zones.
- **Sales Cycle**: 6 - 12 months.
- **Entry Strategy**: RFP participation, executive consulting networks.

---

## 2. Customer Acquisition Channels

- **Channel 1: ERP Partnerships**
  - Partner with regional ERP integrators. Provide them with standard connector commissions to recommend Pharma OS as the field SFA wrapper for their clients.
- **Channel 2: Direct Enterprise Outbound**
  - Custom pilot offers. Run a 30-day sandbox pilot for one territory (10-20 reps) to prove ROI in fraud prevention and order volume growth.
- **Channel 3: Industry Conferences**
  - Host booths at events like CPhI or local digital health conventions, demonstrating real-time AI route optimization.

---

## 3. Product Positioning & Value Proposition

- **The Hook**: *"Reduce field expense leakage by up to 25% within 90 days."* (Appeals directly to CFOs / HR managers).
- **The Engine**: *"Visit the right doctor at the right time with the AI Doctor Intelligence Index."* (Appeals directly to Sales Directors).
- **The Shield**: *"Biometric face recognition and strict device locking guarantees field attendance compliance."* (Appeals to Operations / Security teams).
